# Myelin — Permanent Publishing & Sustainability Model

*Continuation of `myelin-collaboration-discovery-and-indexing-architecture.md`.*

This document records only the new plans discussed afterward.

---

## 1. Public Publishing Is Permanent

Once a work is published on Myelin, it remains publicly available even if the author's publishing subscription later expires.

The subscription buys the ability to publish new work during an active subscription period. It does **not** function as rent on already-published work.

If an author stops subscribing:

- existing public publications remain online;
- existing URLs remain valid;
- profiles, collaboration records, and attribution remain intact;
- published work continues to participate in Myelin's archive and discovery system.

If the author later wants to publish new work, they can renew the publishing subscription.

The model is therefore:

**create something worth publishing  
→ activate publishing access  
→ publish it  
→ leave it online permanently  
→ renew only when there is new work worth publishing**

---

## 2. Myelin as a Permanent High-Quality Archive

Permanent publication changes the role of the public platform.

Myelin is intended to become a durable archive of high-quality work rather than a feed whose contents depend on authors continuously paying rent.

The archive can accumulate:

- research;
- essays;
- visual work;
- experiments;
- interactive publications;
- teaching material;
- collaborations;
- datasets;
- technical investigations;
- other serious work.

This is especially important for work that may be cited, referenced, taught from, or discovered years later.

---

## 3. Public Publishing Is Primarily a Distribution and Reputation Engine

The public side of Myelin does not need to be the dominant recurring revenue source.

Its primary roles are:

- attracting readers;
- showcasing Myelin's publishing capabilities;
- building author and contributor profiles;
- enabling collaboration;
- creating a high-quality permanent archive;
- growing search authority through selectively indexed work;
- establishing Myelin as a place associated with serious work;
- bringing some readers and authors into the private Myelin product.

Public publishing can therefore optimize for quality and permanence rather than maximizing recurring billing.

---

## 4. Private Sharing and Collaboration Remain Recurring

Private sharing is different from public publishing because its value is continuously provided.

Private Myelin can include ongoing services such as:

- private document sharing;
- shared research material;
- private group workspaces;
- live collaboration;
- sync;
- access control;
- permissions;
- comments and discussion;
- shared notes;
- collaborative writing and research.

These services naturally justify a recurring subscription because the service continues to operate while the group is using it.

---

## 5. Private Collaboration Is Expected to Be the Main Revenue Engine

The working business assumption is that **private sharing and collaboration will become the dominant source of recurring revenue**.

Researchers have recurring reasons to share private documents and work with collaborators.

Students may also need private sharing and collaboration for group projects, shared notes, study groups, coursework, research, and collaborative writing.

Teachers, research groups, labs, and institutions may eventually create larger recurring use cases.

The economic split is therefore:

### Public Myelin

**Permanent archive + discovery + reputation + collaboration + distribution**

### Private Myelin

**Recurring utility + sharing + sync + live collaboration + managed infrastructure**

The public side grows the network.

The private side is expected to finance ongoing operation.

---

## 6. The Two Sides Reinforce Each Other

The intended loop becomes:

**excellent permanent public work  
→ high-quality readers  
→ new authors, creators, students, and researchers discover Myelin  
→ some begin using Myelin privately  
→ private sharing and collaboration become part of their ongoing workflow  
→ recurring subscriptions support Myelin  
→ continued development improves both the private product and public publishing system  
→ more excellent work is published permanently**

This allows the public archive to remain quality-focused without forcing every public author to become a long-term recurring subscriber.

---

## 7. Permanent Publications Continue Creating Value After Cancellation

An author who subscribes briefly can still leave lasting value behind.

For example:

- an author subscribes for a publishing period;
- publishes several strong works;
- later cancels;
- those publications remain available;
- readers continue discovering them;
- collaborators continue receiving attribution;
- the works continue strengthening Myelin's archive;
- indexed works can continue contributing to Myelin's search presence and reputation.

The author's subscription may stop, but the work can continue bringing readers and future users into Myelin.

---

## 8. Six-Month Sustainability Goal

A six-month sustainability target is plausible if the goal is:

> **Myelin covers its own infrastructure and operating costs and develops a small stable base of recurring private users.**

This is different from requiring Myelin to replace a full-time salary within six months.

Useful early signals would be:

- public publications producing new users;
- private users inviting collaborators;
- invited collaborators becoming independent users;
- contributors using their complimentary publishing access;
- collaboration loops beginning without direct founder intervention;
- some users continuing to pay specifically for private sharing, sync, and collaboration.

If these loops begin working at small scale, Myelin can be considered commercially alive even before revenue becomes large.

---

## 9. Sustainability Does Not Require Mass Adoption

The model is intentionally compatible with a relatively small high-quality user base.

Because Myelin is local-first and public publishing is intended to be inexpensive to serve, the project does not need mass-market adoption before it can become sustainable.

A relatively small number of recurring private subscribers can support infrastructure, managed sync, collaboration services, continued development, and maintenance of the permanent public archive.

This allows Myelin to optimize the public ecosystem for quality rather than volume.

---

## 10. Distilled Business Architecture

**Public work is permanent.**

**A publishing subscription allows new works to be published during an active period.**

**Cancelling does not remove previously published work.**

**Authors renew when they want to publish again.**

**The public platform builds discovery, reputation, collaboration, and a permanent archive.**

**Private sharing, sync, and live collaboration remain recurring services.**

**Private collaboration is expected to become the primary recurring revenue source.**

**The public archive attracts the people who may later become private users.**

**The private product finances the system that keeps the archive and wider Myelin ecosystem sustainable.**
