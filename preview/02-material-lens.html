§4.2 Variance decomposition across the harness grid

We evaluate 2 model sizes × 2 harness conditions, 3 seeds, 240 tasks each.

Holding the model fixed, the harness term dominates. In the naive condition the failure mode is uniformly schema adherence under multi-step chains — the model selects the correct tool and emits an ill-typed argument.

This is not a reasoning failure and does not scale with parameters: the 20B naive condition trails the 8B instrumented condition by 14 points.

§5 Scaffolding ablation across four model families

Peer-reviewed. Independent harness, independent task set, independent eval harness.

We observe a crossover below 10B parameters: scaffolding quality predicts task completion better than parameter count. Above 14B the two explanations are statistically indistinguishable in our setup.

We do not claim scaffolding quality is universally dominant. Our evaluation does not include long-horizon tool chains, and we do not vary the tool schema's typing.
