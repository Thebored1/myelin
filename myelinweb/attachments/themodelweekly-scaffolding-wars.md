# The new scaffolding wars

3 Feb 2026. The Model Weekly.

## The observation

Several vendors said, separately and in near-identical language this month, that the distance between a strong agent harness and a weak one has been widening faster than the distance between one model generation and the next.

Nobody published a number. The observation is stated as a matter of operational experience, and the phrase used across at least three separate announcements was that the scaffold is becoming the product.

The convergence of unrelated teams on the same claim is itself the newsworthy part. These are competitors, on different stacks, with different incentives.

## What each vendor actually reported

Every announcement framed its own harness as a fixed condition. The comparison was always outbound: our configuration against a competitor's published numbers, at whatever scaffold the competitor happened to publish with.

> In not one case did a vendor hold their own scaffold constant, vary the competitor's, or rerun a competitor's configuration under a second scaffold.

The numbers therefore cannot be composed into a picture of the harness effect. They answer the question each vendor chose to answer.

## The missing variable

The claim is directionally reasonable and evidentially thin. To measure the gap between a strong and a weak harness you need two harnesses run over the same tasks, and nothing in this reporting cycle supplies that.

There is a related gap in the other direction. The claim is about harnesses, and it is being made by people who sell harnesses, with no scaffold manipulation anywhere in the evidence.

## Provenance note

This piece is background reading. It establishes that the industry has noticed the harness effect and that vendors now advertise against it. It isolates no variables, publishes no controlled comparison, and supports no causal claim.

It is not cited in the manuscript. It is retained because a reader encountering the paper's central claim should know it is not news, and that the news cycle around it is running ahead of the controlled evidence.
