# Small models are catching up

12 Jan 2026. The Model Weekly.

## The numbers

Gap-finder runs published this week show the distance between 7B-class and 30B-class models narrowing on tool-use benchmarks. On three of the five suites in the current composite, a 7B-class model now lands within a few points of a 30B-class model that was ahead by double digits a year earlier.

The composite vendor describes the change as consistent rather than dramatic. It is consistent in the sense that it appears across suites, families, and prompt formats, and unremarkable in the sense that no single cell moved enough on its own to be newsworthy.

## Why it is happening

Nobody in the reporting is offering a single account, and the explanations on offer are not the same as each other.

Training data has grown for small models specifically, because the last two rounds of capability work has been aimed at tool calling rather than at general fluency. Distillation from larger teachers into small students has become routine. Quantised serving has moved past the point where a 7B model in production is a noticeably degraded object. And the benchmarks themselves have drifted toward tasks that smaller models are structurally well suited to.

Each of these is a factor. None of them is isolated, and the reporting does not attempt to isolate them.

## What the reporting does not do

> No comparison in this piece varies one thing and holds the rest fixed. There is no rerun of anyone's published configuration anywhere in the story.

Every number reported here is a vendor's own number, run on the vendor's own stack, against a task definition the vendor assembled. That is normal and it is not fraud. It does mean the direction is well attested and the magnitude is not.

## Provenance note

This item is background reading. It establishes the direction of travel — that the small-model and large-model gap on tool use is closing — and it says nothing about why. It is not cited in the manuscript and no claim in the paper rests on it. It is included because a reader who has not followed the 7B-versus-30B comparison needs to know the baseline is not flat, and this piece is an adequate source for that one fact.
